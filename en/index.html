FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What if we have no data to train the model on?

We start with what you already have in your accounting system (tills, CRM, warehouse). That is usually enough for a first working version. The model keeps learning as data accumulates during the pilot.

What happens to our data?

Your data stays yours and is not passed to third parties. For manufacturing tasks an on-premise mode is possible, so data never leaves your site.

What if the solution does not pay back?

Target KPIs are fixed in the contract before work begins. After rollout we transparently show the actual effect on the client's data.

How long does rollout take?

Audit takes 1-2 days. Setup and integration take from 3 days to 2-3 weeks depending on scale, with a pilot at one location before full launch.

What does support include?

24/7 support, retraining the model on new data, improvements as the business grows. Terms depend on the plan.
