Automating a Restaurant with Neural Networks

How artificial intelligence takes over reservations, purchasing, and kitchen control — and what this delivers for a restaurant in numbers.

Running a restaurant means a daily fight against unpredictability: today there's a queue for reservations, tomorrow half the room is empty, and in the kitchen you're either short on ingredients or writing off expired ones. For a long time, the only answer to this unpredictability was a manager's experience and intuition. Now artificial intelligence is increasingly taking over that role — and doing it more accurately, because it sees the data instead of guessing.

What "Automating a Restaurant with Neural Networks" Means

This isn't about a robot waiter or a chatbot that just replies with canned phrases. Automating a restaurant with neural networks means a set of connected AI modules that work with the venue's real data: POS receipts, reservations, warehouse stock, and guest order history. The model trains on this data and starts predicting demand, suggesting purchases, and monitoring kitchen processes more accurately than can be done by hand.

In the ST8 AI solution for HoReCa, this is implemented as a set of five connected modules: digital host, predictive purchasing, inventory management, menu personalization, and kitchen computer vision.

Digital Host 24/7

The first thing a guest encounters is booking and messaging. The digital host accepts reservations and answers questions on Telegram and WhatsApp around the clock, and — more importantly — remembers regular guests' preferences: allergies, favorite table, birthday. For the restaurant, this means fewer missed bookings outside the host's working hours and more personal service without hiring an extra person for the front desk.

Predictive Purchasing: How AI Cuts Waste

The most expensive mistake a restaurant can make is buying more ingredients than it can sell. Predictive purchasing forecasts demand 7 days ahead, factoring in the day of the week, seasonality, and recent order trends, and tells you exactly how much of each ingredient to buy. Across ST8 AI's HoReCa clients, this delivers an average 35% reduction in write-offs — money that used to be literally thrown away now stays in the register.

Inventory Management and Menu Personalization

Alongside purchasing, the system tracks the shelf life of ingredients already in stock and works on a FIFO basis, automatically alerting staff to what needs to be used first. And based on a guest's order history, AI suggests to the waiter — or to the guest directly in the app — which dishes to offer, increasing the average check without any pushy upselling.

Computer Vision in the Kitchen

In the kitchen, the neural network acts as an extra supervisor: it monitors portion control, cleanliness of work areas, and cooking standards in real time via cameras. This is especially valuable for restaurant chains, where quality needs to stay consistent across every location, and no chef can physically stand at every station at once.

Results

According to ST8 AI's HoReCa clients, a full rollout of these modules delivers:

-35%Write-offs
+17%Avg check
-40%No-show

A live example of this kind of rollout is the ST8 Dark Intelligence case for Azbuka Vkusa, where AI production planning forecasts peak hours and optimizes batch cooking, and the CHEFF AI case for Unik Food, where the neural network took over nutritional calculations, process specs, and HACCP compliance monitoring.

Where to Start

Rollout starts with integrating your POS system — iiko or R-Keeper — which takes about 3 days. AI then trains on the venue's real data: menu, sales, reservations. Within a week, the system predicts demand more accurately than a manager with years of experience. The pilot launches at a single location — full deployment across the whole chain happens only after the result is confirmed in practice.
