AI concierge for restaurants and hotels

Test a guest-assistance workflow at one restaurant, hotel or hospitality location. ST8 AI starts with approved information and a clear staff handover, then agrees how the pilot will be measured before it goes live.

The problem: a helpful reply is only part of the task

A concierge workflow can involve a question, a booking request and an action by staff. Before automating it, we need to know where the information comes from and who completes the task. During the audit, we follow this path at the chosen location instead of assuming that a conversation alone is the result.

The pilot should therefore have a small, understandable remit. Choose the enquiry types, identify the approved answers and agree when a person takes over. This gives staff a way to review the assistant's work and keeps the evaluation attached to a real process at one site.

What we put inside the agreed boundary

Answers grounded in your material

The proposed assistant uses the information selected for the pilot. We agree who owns that material and which questions it can support. Missing information becomes a reason to ask for clarification or hand over to staff; it does not become a new promise about the restaurant or hotel.

Booking and enquiry handovers

The proposed concierge scope covers selected booking enquiries and messenger questions. The audit determines the actual channel and connection available at your location. We describe how an enquiry reaches a responsible person and where the outcome is recorded, so the workflow has an end beyond sending a message.

Reviewable exceptions

We agree which requests need human attention and how they are shown to the team. Staff should be able to identify the original enquiry, understand the proposed next step and record what happened. This is the part of the pilot that connects a guest-facing assistant with an operational process.

How the one-site concierge pilot works

Map the current conversation

Choose one location and one set of enquiries. Establish the sources staff already use, the handover points and the person who will review exceptions. Record the starting position and agree the indicators that will be measured.

Set the access and action rules

ST8 AI is an AI layer over existing systems and spreadsheets. Available exports or APIs are checked during the audit. The pilot agreement sets what is connected, what the assistant can communicate and which actions require staff review. Scope and price follow that discussion.

Run a limited experiment

The proposed timetable provides for launch in about 7–10 days and roughly 3–4 weeks of operation, subject to scope and data access. KPIs and the measurement method are fixed in the contract. Compare the agreed indicators and review the exceptions before considering wider use.

Start with one location, not an open-ended brief

Send us the enquiry channel, a short description of the task and the current system. We can then discuss an assistant whose job is concrete enough to test. This page proposes a pilot model; it does not claim that a particular hotel or restaurant already achieved a saving. The decision to expand should follow the evidence from the agreed location and workflow.

Frequently asked questions

Why begin with one location?

One location and a defined enquiry group give the pilot a clear boundary for measurement and staff review.

Which information can the concierge use?

The information sources and supported questions are agreed in the audit, with a responsible owner for the content.

How are unclear enquiries handled?

The workflow defines when to ask for clarification and when to hand the request to the responsible person.

What is agreed before launch?

The location, sources, access, handovers, timetable and KPI measurement method are set out for the proposed pilot.

Does a pilot establish results for other locations?

Its evidence applies to the agreed workflow. Review the actual records and limitations before discussing a wider scope.
