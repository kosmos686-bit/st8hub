The problem: the answer and the handover belong to different people

A guest question only becomes a completed task when the right information or action reaches the guest. During the audit, we examine where enquiries arrive, who answers them and what happens when a request needs another member of staff. We establish the actual workflow before deciding what should be automated.

For a hotel, the first decision is therefore the scope of the conversation. Which questions can be answered from approved material? Which need staff attention? An assistant is easier to evaluate when these boundaries are explicit and its output can be connected to the action that follows.

What the first assistant can cover

Approved guest information

An AI guest assistant for a hotel starts with material the property agrees to use. The pilot defines the information sources and the questions in scope. If an answer is not supported by those sources, the planned action is a handover to staff. New services or policies are not invented to complete a conversation.

Enquiry routing

We follow one agreed enquiry type from its arrival to the staff action: receive the enquiry, analyse the available information and prepare an action for the responsible person. The audit establishes where the handover is delivered and how staff record its outcome.

A bounded agent workflow

AI agents for hospitality should have a defined job and a clear stopping point. In the first pilot, we agree what the assistant can communicate, what needs review and which systems it can use. That makes the proposed workflow assessable without assuming that an agent can make unrestricted operational decisions.

How a single-property pilot is organised

Select the enquiry category

Choose one property and a limited set of guest questions or requests. Identify the people who own the approved information and the staff actions. Record the present workflow and agree the indicators to compare after the pilot.

Connect the available information

ST8 AI works above existing systems and spreadsheets. During the audit, we check the exports or APIs available for the chosen workflow and agree the access boundary. A named system on a website is not enough to establish a ready connection for a specific hotel.

Operate, compare and decide

The proposed timetable is about 7–10 days to launch and roughly 3–4 weeks of operation, depending on scope and data access. KPIs and their measurement method are written into the contract before launch. Review guest enquiries, handovers and exceptions against the agreed baseline before deciding on another process or property.

Bring one workflow to the discussion

Send a short description of the enquiry channel, the information staff currently consult and the team that handles exceptions. Scope and price can then be discussed around that workflow. Frequently asked questions

What should the first hotel pilot cover?

One property and a limited enquiry or request category, with approved information and a named staff handover.

What happens when the assistant lacks an answer?

The proposed workflow asks for clarification or hands the enquiry to staff rather than inventing hotel information.

Can an agent make unrestricted decisions?

The pilot defines its access and communication limits, including the actions that need staff review.

How are hotel results measured?

Record the chosen workflow baseline and agree the indicators and comparison method in the contract before launch.
