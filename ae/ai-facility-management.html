The problem: a request is recorded, but the next action is unclear

A useful starting point is the handover between an incoming request and the person who must act on it. During the audit, we examine how requests arrive, how a team is selected and where completion is recorded. We establish what actually happens in your operation before choosing an automation scope.

Facilities management AI automation should connect those steps. A dashboard alone does not settle who owns a task, which information is missing or whether someone has reviewed an exception. The first pilot therefore follows a request through the existing process, rather than introducing another disconnected queue.

What we do with your existing information

Request intake and routing

We agree which request types enter the pilot and what information is needed to route them. The proposed workflow classifies the request, identifies missing details and prepares a handover for the responsible team. Your staff retain the agreed decision points; unclear cases are included in the review process.

Documents and reporting

For the FM pilot, identify the reports, exports or spreadsheets your team can provide and agree which information supports the selected request category. The output must help a responsible person act on a request or exception, rather than merely produce another summary.

A traceable action

We map the path from source information to analysis, recommendation and responsible action. The scope defines where the recommendation appears and how its outcome is recorded. AI for FM companies becomes testable when this final step is visible in the same process used to establish the starting position.

How the one-building pilot works

Audit the workflow

Select one building, one request category and the systems involved. Agree data access, the responsible team and the proposed KPI definitions. Record the current process before making changes so the pilot has a usable baseline.

Connect the agreed sources

ST8 AI works as a layer over existing ERP, CRM, document systems and spreadsheets. The audit determines which exports or APIs are available. The pilot agreement sets the connection scope; it does not assume every system can be connected without preparation.

Operate and review

The proposed timetable allows about 7–10 days for launch and roughly 3–4 weeks of operation, subject to the agreed scope and data access. KPIs and the measurement method go into the contract. Review exceptions and staff actions during the pilot, then compare the agreed indicators with the baseline before deciding on wider use.

Prepare a concrete first discussion

Bring a short description of one building workflow, the current system and an example of the information your team works with. We can then discuss the input, handover and output of a limited pilot. Scope and price are agreed after the audit; this page does not assign a generic saving to your building.

Which KPIs are used?
