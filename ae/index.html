FAQ

Questions UAE operators ask us

How does an AI pilot with ST8 AI work?

We start with a short audit of one costly process, connect to the data you already have, and launch a pilot on a single site in about 7 to 10 days. The pilot runs for roughly 3 to 4 weeks, and the target KPIs are written into the contract before launch.

Do we have to replace our POS, ERP or CRM?

No. ST8 AI works as an AI layer on top of the systems you already use and connects through their existing exports or APIs.

What does a pilot cost?

Scope and price depend on the process and the systems involved, so we agree them after a short audit instead of publishing a single price.

What happens to our data?

Your data stays yours and is not shared with third parties. For sensitive environments an on-premise deployment is possible.

Can we stop if the pilot does not work?

Yes. Our contracts include a 30-day exit without penalty, and full rollout only starts after a successful single-site pilot.

Where is the team based?

ST8 AI is a Moscow-based team. We work with UAE operators remotely, and on-site work can be agreed per project.

Tell us one process that costs you money

Describe it in two or three lines. We will reply with whether a pilot makes sense and what it would involve.
